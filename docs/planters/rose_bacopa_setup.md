# Rose/Bacopa Planter Setup

Current setup and care reference for the 2026 Knock Out Rose + White Bacopa
containers.

## Active Planters

- Three main front rose/bacopa planters
- One south-side experimental rose/bacopa planter with a self-watering insert

All four planters contain:

- 1 Petite Knock Out Rose, purchased in a 2.5 qt container
- 2 smaller White Bacopa plants, approximately 1 qt each
- Triangular planting layout, with bacopa facing outward

## Main Front Planters

The three main front planters use:

- Miracle-Gro Moisture Control planter mix blended 50:50 with last year's soil
- 6 inches of styrofoam in the bottom
- Landscape fabric above the styrofoam
- No added fertilizer, iron, sulfur, inoculant, or other amendments yet

Two front planters get morning sun. The south-end front planter gets most-day sun
and should dry faster.

## South-Side Experiment

The south-side planter uses:

- A slightly taller cement composite planter
- A 3 gal Proven Winners AquaPots Lite self-watering insert
- Two added wick pieces
- No added fertilizer, iron, sulfur, inoculant, or other amendments yet

The reservoir is currently being filled twice weekly and is not running out of
water. That confirms reservoir capacity is adequate so far, but it does not prove
the upper root zone is at the right moisture level.

## Watering

For all rose/bacopa planters, check moisture about 2 inches below the soil surface.
Use that root-zone check to decide whether top watering is needed.

For the south-side experiment:

- Keep filling the reservoir before it runs dry
- Also check moisture 2 inches below the soil surface
- Top-water periodically if the upper mix is dry even when the reservoir still has
  water
- Treat reservoir level and root-zone moisture as separate checks

The first two weeks after planting had top watering 1-2 times per day during
establishment.

## Bacopa Reset

If bacopa was planted too low, lift the whole bacopa plug/root ball, add fresh mix
underneath, and reset it so the plug surface is level with or slightly above the
finished mix.

Do not fix low bacopa by burying the crown or lower stems from above.

After resetting, top-water to settle the plug and recheck appearance and moisture
after 3-7 days. Recheck the south-side experiment again 7-14 days farther into the
experiment.

## Feeding

No fertilizer, iron, sulfur, inoculant, or other amendments have been added yet.
When feeding starts, keep rose fertilizer at the lower end because bacopa shares
the container.
