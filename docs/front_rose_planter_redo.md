# Front Rose Planters Redo

Actionable setup guide for the three front garage planters.

## Goal

Redo the three front garage accent planters for 2026 by:

- replacing all three Knock Out Roses
- replacing candytuft with White Bacopa 'Snowstorm Giant Snowflake'
- removing the heavy pea-gravel base
- rebuilding with a lighter, more movable setup for future winter storage

## Plant Count Per Pot

Default layout per planter:

- 1 Knock Out Rose
- 2 White Bacopa plants

Optional fuller look:

- 1 Knock Out Rose
- 3 White Bacopa plants if the starts are small and you want faster fill

## Materials

On hand or reuse first:

- existing Earth Science elemental sulfur
- existing Liquid Ironite for provisional container iron support
- spare nursery pots for filler structure
- clean non-biodegradable EPS from packages
- landscape fabric

Buy:

- 2 bags of Miracle-Gro Organic Choice Potting Mix with Compost, 50 qt

## Reuse The Existing Soil

Default approach:

- reuse the best existing potting mix
- blend old mix with new mix
- do not try to rescue tired mix mainly with fertilizer

Use the old mix if it is still:

- easy to rewet
- not sour-smelling
- not compacted into sludge
- not heavily crusted with salts

Good default blend:

- about 50:50 saved old mix and fresh new mix

Lean more toward new mix in the hottest pot if it has degraded the most.

## Step-By-Step

1. Remove dead flowers and any loose dead roots or debris.
2. Remove the old rose and old root ball.
3. Remove and discard the pea-gravel base.
4. Save the best existing potting mix from the upper and middle root zone.
5. Discard mix that is sour, muddy, badly compacted, or strongly hydrophobic.
6. Put upside-down nursery pots in the bottom 3-5 inches of the planter.
7. Fill around them with broken non-biodegradable EPS.
8. Lay landscape fabric above the filler layer.
9. Blend saved old mix with fresh new mix.
10. Add a small amount of elemental sulfur to the refreshed upper mix.
11. Optionally add water-storage polymer crystals at label rate.
12. Optionally add a small amount of DYNOMYCO in the refreshed planting zone.
13. Plant the new rose slightly high, not buried low.
14. Fill around the rose with the refreshed mix and water thoroughly.
15. After frost risk passes, plant bacopa around the outer edge.
16. Add a light 1-2 inch mulch layer on top.

## Watering Strategy

Core rule:

- water when dry about 2 inches down

Expectations:

- the hottest pot will need water sooner than the others
- all three should perform better than before once the gravel is removed, mix is
  refreshed, and mulch is added

Ways to get more time between waterings:

- keep the filler layer shallow
- mulch the top surface
- let bacopa shade the soil
- use the moisture-retentive container mix above
- use polymer crystals conservatively if needed
- cluster pots as tightly as practical

Do not rely on:

- buried improvised reservoirs
- watering globes or spikes as the main fix
- rock topdressing
- blocked drainage or standing water

## Feeding Strategy

- Use Jack's Classic Soluble Petunia FeED 20-6-22 as the main container fertilizer
- Keep rates toward the lower end because bacopa shares the pot
- Use existing Liquid Ironite first if midsummer chlorosis shows up
- Do not buy or substitute a high-phosphorus bloom booster

## When To Escalate To A Bigger Rebuild

Switch to mostly new mix and expect more bags if:

- the old mix resists rewetting
- the root ball is badly congested
- drainage is poor and structure is spent
- there is not much usable mix left after removing the gravel
