# Other Planter Planting Concepts

Planting concepts for the remaining patio, barrel, and edible containers.

## What This Guide Covers

- `3` long patio planters behind the sectional: screening-focused
- `1` lower patio long planter: more decorative
- `1` half whiskey barrel: statement ornamental
- `1` self-watering raised bed: mixed edible planting

These concepts assume Zone 5b, full sun for the patio containers, and occasional
dry-down in the manual-watered pots.

Current fill context:

- the four long patio planters use Miracle-Gro potting mix
- the whiskey barrel and self-watering raised bed currently use leftover
  compost/dirt mix from the 2025 lawn nutrition, leveling, and overseeding
  coverage project

Current planted status as of June 18, 2026:

- Three Veradek long boxes each hold two ornamental grasses. Grass ID is pending;
  current best guess is Karl Foerster feather reed grass or Pink Muhly Grass.
- The lower-patio Veradek long box, after the 2 ft step-down, was put out June 18
  with two English lavender plants and one Salvia 'May Night'. It may receive less
  afternoon shade than the raised-patio planters.
- The whiskey barrel currently holds a lily that was in a small container in 2025.
  It appears to have survived winter but is slow to start in 2026.
- The raised garden bed currently holds a strawberry plant, tomato plant, bell
  pepper, Coolapeno jalapeno-type pepper, purple basil, and oregano.

## Three Long Planters Behind The Sectional

### Default Recommendation: Repeated Shenandoah Screen

Best fit if the main job is to soften or partly hide the back of the sectional.

Use:

- Shenandoah switchgrass as the main repeated structural plant across all three boxes
- Optional low companion only if needed, kept visually secondary

Why choose it:

- strongest screening effect
- upright habit fits the long box shape
- gives you the switchgrass form with more red seasonal color
- feels more distinct from the Northwind switchgrass already planted elsewhere

### Alternate Option: Switchgrass + Bluebeard

Use:

- Prairie Winds 'Apache Rose' switchgrass
- Dark Knight Bluebeard

Why choose it:

- more bloom and softer mass than grass alone
- more distinct from Northwind than simply repeating the same cultivar
- still fits the durable full-sun palette

### Alternate Option: Xeric Texture Mix

Use:

- Blonde Ambition Blue Grama
- Autumn Joy Sedum
- optional Agastache accent

Why choose it:

- strongest dry-down tolerance
- good if softening matters more than true screening

## Lower Patio Long Planter

### Default Recommendation: Decorative Echo Box

Best fit if you want it to coordinate with the screening trio but still feel nicer
and more intentional up close.

Use:

- Shenandoah switchgrass
- White Bacopa 'Snowstorm Giant Snowflake' or another heat-tolerant trailing annual
- optional low sedum for added texture

Why choose it:

- matches the screening boxes without looking flat
- gives the visible planter a cleaner decorative finish

### Alternate Option: Bluebeard Feature Box

Use:

- Dark Knight Bluebeard
- Autumn Joy Sedum or Blonde Ambition Blue Grama

Why choose it:

- more flower and seasonal interest
- still grounded in the existing property palette

### Alternate Option: Pollinator Color Box

Use:

- Agastache Sunset Hyssop
- Blonde Ambition Blue Grama
- optional trailing annual edge

Why choose it:

- most colorful and wildlife-friendly of the long-box options

## Whiskey Barrel

The barrel should be treated as a statement ornamental container, not as a reliable
overwinter experiment. A larger pot may buffer roots somewhat, but it does not make
outdoor overwintering dependable in this climate.

### Default Recommendation: Bold Summer Statement

Use:

- Purple Majesty millet or another bold upright annual grass
- White Bacopa or calibrachoa for edge softening
- optional second hot-sun accent for more color

Why choose it:

- strongest first-year visual impact
- embraces the barrel as a summer focal piece

### Alternate Option: Hardy-Leaning Statement

Use:

- Prairie Winds 'Apache Rose' switchgrass
- Autumn Joy Sedum
- optional trailing annual edge

Why choose it:

- lower water demand
- more structure, less seasonal churn

### Alternate Option: Pollinator Barrel

Use:

- Agastache Sunset Hyssop
- Blonde Ambition Blue Grama
- low sedum or trailing edge annual

Why choose it:

- colorful, drought-tolerant, and aligned with the property's sunny plant palette

## Raised Self-Watering Edible Bed

This planter sits against the south side of the house, which points slightly
southeast. It gets morning and afternoon sun, then late-afternoon shade from the
house, so fruiting crops are still realistic but total heat and production may be
lower than in an unobstructed full-sun site.

### Default Recommendation: Kitchen Staples Bed

Use:

- one determinate or patio tomato
- basil
- parsley
- chives
- thyme or oregano

Why choose it:

- highest everyday kitchen value
- good use of a self-watering bed if sun is strong enough

### Alternate Option: Pepper-And-Herb Bed

Use:

- one or two compact peppers
- basil
- parsley
- thyme or oregano

Why choose it:

- compact and practical
- a little easier to fit neatly than a tomato-led bed

### Alternate Option: Salad-And-Herb Bed

Use:

- cut-and-come greens
- arugula or spinach by season
- parsley
- chives
- dill or cilantro by season

Why choose it:

- best fallback if the site gets less sun
- easiest to harvest in small amounts over time

## Default Set If You Want To Decide Quickly

- Lower patio long box: Decorative Echo Box
- Whiskey barrel: Bold Summer Statement
- Raised edible bed: Kitchen Staples Bed
