| Tool | Best for | Notes |

## Lawn Nitrogen: Why Granular, Not Liquid

Consumer liquid fertilizers (including Simple Lawn Solutions 15-0-15) are supplement
products. At label rates, they deliver approximately **0.05–0.12 lb N per 1,000 sq ft
per application** — 5 to 10 times below the 0.75–1.25 lb N agronomic target for each
primary lawn application. Achieving agronomic rates with liquid would require applying
5–8× the label rate, making it expensive and impractical at lawn scale.

**Liquid 15-0-15 is appropriate for trees and shrubs** (small per-plant volumes in a
bucket, easy to control dosing) but is not practical as the primary lawn N source.

**For the lawn:** use granular ammonium sulfate (21-0-0) or a granular slow-release N
product applied via broadcast spreader. These deliver the full agronomic rate
efficiently. Water in thoroughly after application.

**Annual lawn N target:** 2.75–3.25 lb N/1,000 sq ft total, fall-weighted:

| Application | Rate | Timing |
|-------------|------|--------|
| Spring 1 | 0.75–1.0 lb N/1,000 sq ft | May |
| Spring 2 | 0.5 lb N/1,000 sq ft | June |
| Fall primary | 1.0–1.25 lb N/1,000 sq ft | September — most important |
| Late fall | 0.5 lb N/1,000 sq ft | October |
| **Total** | **2.75–3.25 lb N** | |

## Nitrogen: Quick-Release vs. Slow-Release

**Granular ammonium sulfate (21-0-0)** is quick-release. Works well for spring
applications when bluegrass is actively growing and can use N immediately. Has the
minor side benefit of mild acidification (not meaningful in alkaline clay, but not
harmful). Available at any garden center.

**Slow-release granular** (sulfur-coated urea, polymer-coated urea, or Milorganite) is
preferred for the **September** fall application. Bluegrass "banks" nitrogen in fall for
winter root growth and spring green-up; slow-release delivers this over 6–10 weeks as
soil temperature drops. A slow-release fall fertilizer is the single most impactful
granular substitution if you want to add it.

**Liquid 15-0-15:** Use for trees/shrubs (bucket application); skip for lawn primary N.

## Tank Mixing

### Safe Combinations (can go in the same bucket/sprayer)
- **15-0-15 N-K + LESCO Chelated Micro Mix** — compatible; useful for the May
  combined drip-line bucket pass on trees and shrubs.
- **15-0-15 N-K + humic acid** — compatible.
- **Humic acid + LESCO Chelated Micro Mix** — compatible.
- **Humic acid + lawn fertilizer broadcast** — compatible.

### Apply Solo — Never Mix These
| Product | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **EDDHA iron** (Ferrilene/Sequestrene 138) | Unstable in mixed solution; dedicated bucket drench only |
| **Herbicides** (triclopyr, glyphosate for bindweed) | Never mix with fertilizers; always solo |
| **Compost tea** (EcoCycle Microbe Brew) | Chemical fertilizers kill the living microbes |
| **Anti-desiccant** (Wilt-Pruf/Bonide Wilt-Stop) | Solo foliar application, November only |
| **Insecticidal soap / neem oil / fungicide** | Solo; risk of phytotoxicity in combination |

## Product List by Task

### Iron — EDDHA form (pH-stable; works at pH 7.27)
**Ferrilene 6% EDDHA** or **Sequestrene 138** (equivalent products)
- Water-soluble granular powder — dissolve in 1–2 gallons of water per plant, pour
  slowly at drip line, follow with slow hose soak. **Apply solo — do not mix.**
- Used for: Honeylocust (May + June), Japanese Maple (May + July)
- Where: Online — Amazon, pestrong.com, intermountainturf.com; or SiteOne Landscape Supply
- Not at Home Depot or Ace

### Iron — EDTA form (containers only — acceptable in container mix pH)
**Bonide Liquid Iron** or **Southern Ag Chelated Liquid Iron**
- Used for: Knock Out Rose containers only (monthly May–August)
- Mix in watering can; apply as a drench to container soil
- Where: Ace Hardware, Home Depot, garden centers

### Chelated Micronutrient Package — Full Suite (Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu, Mg, S, Mo)
**LESCO Chelated Micro Mix** (liquid)
- Applied to ALL in-ground trees, shrubs, and perennials in May — highest-priority
  annual task. Mix into bucket with 15-0-15 for a combined per-plant drench pass.
- Application: measured amount per label into a bucket of water; pour at drip line
  per plant, follow with slow hose soak.
- Where: SiteOne Landscape Supply (open to public; Denver/Aurora metro locations)
- Alternative mail-order: Golf Course Lawn Micronutrient Blend (golfcourselawn.store)

### N-K Fertilizer — Phosphorus-Free

**For trees and shrubs:**
**Simple Lawn Solutions 15-0-15** (liquid concentrate; also contains humic acid + kelp)
- Mix measured amount per label into a bucket; pour as drip-line drench per plant
- Can be combined in the same bucket as LESCO Chelated Micro Mix (May combined pass)
- Where: Amazon, simplelawnsolutions.com

**For lawn:**
**Granular ammonium sulfate 21-0-0** (spring applications) or slow-release granular
(fall September application)
- Apply via broadcast spreader at rate to achieve target lb N/1,000 sq ft (see table above)
- Water in thoroughly after application
- Where: Ace Hardware, Home Depot

### Acidifying Fertilizer (Japanese Maple + Mugo Pine only)
**Ammonium sulfate 21-0-0** (granular) — mild acidification as a side effect of N
- Broadcast at drip line and water in thoroughly
- Where: Ace Hardware, Home Depot
- Note: Holly-tone is the single-product convenience alternative but contains some P

### Rose Container Fertilizer (every 3–4 weeks, April–mid-August)
**Jack's Classic Rose FeED 12-4-16** (water-soluble powder, mix into solution)
- Low-P relative to older rose fertilizer formulations; high K supports repeat bloom
- Mix per label into a watering can; apply to container soil
- Where: Amazon, specialty garden centers

### Humic/Fulvic Acid — Spring Broadcast
**Simple Lawn Solutions Humic Acid** or **BioAg TM-7** (liquid concentrate)
- April broadcast to all beds and lawn via hose-end sprayer
- Builds organic matter over seasons; complementary to chelated micronutrient program
- Not a pH amendment — does not lower soil pH
- Where: Amazon

### Compost Tea
**EcoCycle Microbe Brew** — local Boulder, CO product (ecocycle.org)
- Apply same day as purchase (viable microbial counts drop within 24–48 hrs)
- Spring (April/May): hose-end for lawn; bucket slow-pour at tree/shrub drip lines
- Fall (September): after core aeration and compost topdressing
- Do NOT apply in summer (clay surface temps kill microbes before establishment)

### Wetting Agent / Summer Irrigation Efficiency
**Revive Ready Spray** (Colorado-specific; wetting agent + humic + iron)
- Attach to hose — no separate sprayer needed
- June application; follow immediately with irrigation
- Where: Ace Hardware, Home Depot — carried at Colorado stores
- Concentrate also available (1-gal jug) for hose-end sprayer use

### Pre-Emergent — Lawn (keep granular)
**Scotts Halts** (pendimethalin) or prodiamine granular
- Granular is more practical for 2,500 sq ft lawn: even coverage, longer residual
- Apply via broadcast spreader at forsythia bloom (~late March, soil at 50°F) and again in August
- Where: Home Depot, Ace Hardware

### Pre-Emergent — Beds and Rock Areas
Liquid pre-emergent (Preen Liquid or prodiamine concentrate) via M12 sprayer
- March application, second in April or August if needed

### Potassium Supplement — Fall Trees/Shrubs (keep granular)
**Granular 0-0-50 Potassium Sulfate** (SOP form — sulfate, not chloride/muriate)
- Liquid potassium products are agricultural-scale only; granular is practical here
- Broadcast at each plant's drip line in September, water in immediately
- Where: Ace Hardware, Home Depot

### Lawn Winterizer — Late October (keep granular)
Granular K-heavy winterizer — **Scotts Turf Builder WinterGuard** or equivalent
- Granular slow-release K going into dormancy; more effective than liquid at this timing
- Where: Home Depot, Ace Hardware

### Bindweed Herbicide (late August — spot treatment)
**Triclopyr concentrate** (Ortho Brush-B-Gon or similar) — selective broadleaf
- Apply via M12 sprayer, targeted spot treatment only
- Late August timing reaches roots before dormancy; optimal kill rate
- Where: Home Depot, Ace Hardware

### Anti-Desiccant Spray (late November — Juniper + Blue Spruce only)
**Bonide Wilt-Stop** or **Wilt-Pruf** concentrate
- Mix per label, apply via M12 sprayer as foliar coat on foliage
- Apply solo; do not mix with anything
- Where: Ace Hardware, Home Depot, garden centers

### Pest/Disease (as needed)
- **Insecticidal soap**: Garden Safe or Bonide — M12 sprayer, apply solo
- **Neem oil**: Bonide Neem Oil — M12 sprayer, apply solo
- **Fungicide** (rose black spot): myclobutanil or tebuconazole — M12 sprayer, apply solo
