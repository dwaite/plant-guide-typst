# 2026 Plant Care Journal

Operational record for the 2026 season. This is an internal evidence layer for
what actually happened on the property: applications, watering, mowing, plant
observations, missed or uncertain tasks, and follow-up checks.

This journal records known facts and explicit observations. Do not use it to
replace the guide's canonical data files or to add new recommendations directly
to product-facing content. If a season review changes guidance, reconcile that
separately in the appropriate `data/`, `notes/`, or `content/` layer.

## How To Log

Minimum fields for new entries:

- **Date:** exact date, or clearly marked approximate date.
- **Category:** lawn, trees/shrubs, containers, weeds, irrigation, soil biology,
  observation, or another specific operational category.
- **Action/product:** what was done or observed.
- **Amount/rate/dilution:** product amount, application rate, dilution, or "not
  recorded" if unknown.
- **Target:** lawn area, plant list, container, bed, or zone.
- **Water-in/weather context:** irrigation, rain, temperature, wind, or relevant
  moisture context.
- **Observed response/follow-up:** response seen later, or a date/window to check.
- **Confidence:** exact, approximate, or reconstructed.

Journal rules:

- Record only actual work or explicit observations in the chronological log.
- Use approximate dates when needed, and mark them clearly.
- Keep rates in both practical product terms and agronomic terms when known,
  especially for lawn nitrogen.
- Preserve uncertainty instead of smoothing it over.
- Do not convert observations into recommendations inside this file.
