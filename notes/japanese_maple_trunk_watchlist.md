# Japanese Maple Trunk Damage Watchlist

Use this for weekly checks over the next 2-3 weeks after budbreak begins.

## Check Weekly

- [ ] Buds swelling normally across most of the canopy
- [ ] Buds opening on both damaged and less-damaged sides
- [ ] Leaf-out looks reasonably even, without a whole side staying bare
- [ ] No major branches staying dry or failing to push growth
- [ ] Bark around the wound is stable rather than peeling back further
- [ ] Wound margins are not expanding
- [ ] No obvious wilting, undersized leaves, or tip dieback after leaf-out

## If a Branch Looks Questionable

- [ ] Do a small scratch test on a twig or small branch only
- [ ] Green cambium = alive
- [ ] Brown/tan dry tissue = dead

## Do Not Do Yet

- [ ] Do not wrap now
- [ ] Do not prune aggressively before leaf-out is clear
- [ ] Do not apply wound sealer
- [ ] Do not increase fertilizer because of the damage

## Reassuring Signs

- [ ] Most of the canopy leafs out
- [ ] Only minor twig loss
- [ ] Trunk wound stops worsening

## Problem Signs

- [ ] A major scaffold or one whole side does not leaf out
- [ ] Bark damage continues to expand or slough
- [ ] Clear dieback appears above the wound
- [ ] Trunk injury appears to affect a large share of circumference

## After the Watch Period

- [ ] If leaf-out is mostly normal: continue monitoring and reassess whether winter trunk protection is needed next season
- [ ] If dead wood is obvious: prune only after live growth is clear
- [ ] If large sections fail: reassess severity sooner because vascular flow may be compromised
