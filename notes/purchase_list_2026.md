### Why Some Purchases Are Deferred

| Reason | Meaning in this plan |
|---|---|
| Timing-driven | The product is stable, but the application window is months away, so there is no operational gain from buying now. |
| Storage-minimizing | The product would likely keep, but delaying reduces clutter and the chance of overbuying. |
| Availability-driven | Seasonal products like winterizer or anti-desiccant are usually easier to buy closer to use. |
| Shelf-life-driven | The product does not store well enough to justify pre-buying (compost tea is the clearest case). |
