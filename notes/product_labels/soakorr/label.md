## Label Facts

- Product: SoakORR Wetting Agent, distributed by Lawn Supply Company.
- Product description: water conditioner and multi-purpose surfactant intended
  to reduce surface tension and improve infiltration rates of rainfall and
  irrigation water.
- Non-plant-food ingredients:
  - Block Co-Polymer: 60.00%
  - Alkyl Polyol: 30.00%
  - Inert Ingredients: 10.00%
- Listed actions include reducing soil carbonate and bicarbonate loads and
  moving water below the soil surface.
- Best-use context listed on label: periods of sustained heat or dry weather;
  prior to and through onset of drought; localized dry spot reduction.
- Compatibility statement: can be mixed with most pesticides and fertilizers.
  Use this only as a label-level compatibility statement; still avoid tank mixes
  when dose control, site targeting, or product-specific compatibility is unclear.

## Directions Captured From Label

- Rate: apply `3-6 fl oz / 1,000 sq ft`.
- Carrier: apply in `2 gal water`, or irrigate immediately after application if
  lower carrier volumes are used.
- Water-in requirement: irrigate in with at least `1/2 inch` of water
  immediately after application.
- Interval: apply monthly beginning at least one month before the onset of stress
  conditions.
- Mowing timing: irrigation should be applied before next mowing to enhance
  product movement into the soil.

## Property Interpretation For 2026

- First pass target: lawn only, not beds, trees, or containers.
- First pass rate: low label rate, `3 fl oz / 1,000 sq ft`.
- Approximate first-pass amount: about `9 fl oz` for the approximately
  `3,000 sq ft` lawn.
- Timing constraint: apply only after 6pm on a legal SACWSD watering day
  (currently Wednesday or Saturday), then immediately run sprinkler zones to
  supply about `1/2 inch` of water.
- Inspection opportunity: use the water-in run to observe overspray, misting,
  runoff, pooling, blocked or tilted heads, dry corners, and uneven coverage.
- Follow-up cadence: do not automatically repeat; reassess field response,
  weather, legal watering windows, and label guidance before another monthly
  pass.
